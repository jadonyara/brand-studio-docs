## A looping background for a website hero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the comp">
    Timeline bar → `preset` → **1920×1080 · 16:9**. Set `dur` to `360` for a
    six-second loop at 60fps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a look">
    Stay in **Freeform** while you explore. Switch to **Editor** once you like it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it loop seamlessly">
    Leave the motion driven by `speed` alone and don't keyframe anything. The
    generators run continuously, so the clip cuts cleanly as long as you don't
    animate a property to a different value at the end than it had at the start.

    If you do keyframe something, give the first and last keyframes the **same
    value** so the loop doesn't jump.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    **EXPORT** → MP4 → 2× (3840×2160) → **Render**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A transparent overlay for After Effects

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on alpha">
    Controls panel → Colour → **alpha bg**. The preview goes checkerboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export ProRes">
    **EXPORT** → **ProRes 4444 · .mov** → **Render**. Keep it short; ProRes 4444
    is roughly 2 GB per 10 seconds at 4K, and the modal will warn you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If it's too long for ProRes">
    Use **PNG sequence** with **Write into a folder** instead. After Effects
    imports a PNG folder as a sequence with alpha intact, and there's no size
    ceiling.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A still for a slide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Match the slide shape">
    `preset` → **1920×1080 · 16:9**, or set a custom `comp` size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Freeze a frame you like">
    In **Freeform**, press <kbd>space</kbd> to pause when something looks good.
    In **Editor**, scrub the playhead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    **PNG** → **Regular** (or **Transparent** to sit it over a coloured slide) →
    2× → **Download**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A logo sting over a moving background

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the background">
    Pick a look you like. Dark backgrounds read best behind the white lockup —
    set `bg` to `#0a0b0f` under Colour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the logo">
    In the **Brand** group at the top of the panel, set `logo` to
    `lockup-white`, `size` to about `0.34`, and leave `x`/`y` at `0.5` for centre.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Animate it in">
    Go to frame 0. Set `opacity` to `0` and `size` to `0.22`, then click the
    stopwatch on each. Move to ~1.5s and set `opacity` to `1` and `size` back to
    `0.34`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ease it">
    Select all four keyframes with <kbd>⌘A</kbd>, right-click and choose
    **Ease out** so it arrives quickly and settles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    **EXPORT** → MP4 → 2× → **Render**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Particles that build up over time

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start empty">
    Go to frame 0 (<kbd>Home</kbd>). Set particle `count` to `0`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Animate it">
    Click the stopwatch next to `count`. Press <kbd>End</kbd>, then drag `count`
    up to `12`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ease the build">
    Right-click the first keyframe → **Ease out**, so they arrive quickly and
    then settle. Press <kbd>G</kbd> to see and fine-tune the curve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  `count` is a whole number, so its graph draws as a staircase. That's accurate —
  particles appear one at a time. The easing controls *when* each one appears.
</Note>
