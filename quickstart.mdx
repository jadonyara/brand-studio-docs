## A still image in one minute

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tool">
    Go to [mintlify-brand-studio.vercel.app](https://mintlify-brand-studio.vercel.app).
    It opens on **Ribbon** in **Editor** mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Freeform">
    Click **freeform** in the mode switch, top left under the generator tabs.
    The timeline disappears — you don't need it for a still.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a look">
    Open the controls panel on the right and move things around. If you'd
    rather explore than fiddle, scroll to the bottom of the panel and hit
    **Randomize** a few times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export it">
    Click **PNG** in the bottom bar. Pick **Regular** or **Transparent**, pick a
    scale, and hit **Download**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## An animated loop in five

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Editor mode">
    Click **editor**. The timeline reappears at the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on a stopwatch">
    In the controls panel, click the small circle to the left of any control —
    say **amp** under Shape. It turns green and the property appears as a track
    in the timeline. That circle is the stopwatch: it means "animate this".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a second keyframe">
    Press <kbd>End</kbd> to jump to the last frame, then drag the **amp** slider
    to a different value. That creates a second keyframe automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it">
    Press <kbd>space</kbd>. The playhead loops and the value animates between
    your two keyframes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Smooth it out">
    Right-click either keyframe diamond in the timeline and choose **Easy ease**.
    The diamond becomes a circle, meaning the curve has been shaped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Click **EXPORT**, leave the format on **MP4**, and hit **Render**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Repeat step 2 for as many controls as you like — colours, particle density,
  speed. Anything with a stopwatch can be animated, and they all animate
  independently.
</Tip>
