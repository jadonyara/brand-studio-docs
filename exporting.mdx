## Stills

Two buttons in the bottom bar:

- **PNG** — asks for background (regular or transparent) and a scale, then
  downloads the frame currently on screen
- **SVG** — vector, exported immediately at the current frame

<Frame caption="The PNG dialog asks whether you want the background or transparency">
  <img src="/images/ui-png.png" alt="The PNG export dialog with regular and transparent background options" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  **SVG exports the ribbon lines only — particles are not included.** They rely
  on gradient strokes the vector exporter doesn't emit. If you need particles,
  use PNG.
</Warning>

## Video and sequences

Click **EXPORT**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ui-export.png" alt="The export dialog with format, scale, destination and range options" />
</Frame>

| Format | Alpha | Speed | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **MP4 · H.264** | No | Fast | Web, Slack, decks, social |
| **ProRes 4444 · .mov** | Yes | Slow | Handing off to After Effects or Premiere |
| **PNG sequence** | Yes | Slow | Long clips, or anything too large for memory |

MP4 is hardware-encoded and quick. The other two render and encode one frame at
a time, so a long 4K clip takes minutes — the progress bar reports where it is,
and **Stop** cancels cleanly.

## Frame rate

Every export renders frame by frame off the timeline. Frame *N* is handed to the
encoder stamped *N ÷ fps* regardless of how long it took to draw, so **the file
is exactly the composition frame rate** even if the live preview was crawling.
Nothing is captured off the screen.

## Resolution

**Scale** is a multiple of your composition size, and the exact pixel dimensions
are shown next to each option.

<Warning>
  The default composition is **1720×1080**, which is not 16:9. Exporting that at
  2× gives **3440×2160** — a hair narrower than UHD.

  For exactly **3840×2160**, set the comp to **1920×1080** using the `preset`
  dropdown in the timeline bar first, then export at 2×.
</Warning>

## Transparency

Turn on **alpha bg** in the controls panel (under Colour). The preview switches
to a checkerboard so you can see what's transparent.

Then pick a format that can carry it: **ProRes 4444** or a **PNG sequence**.

<Note>
  MP4 has no alpha channel. If **alpha bg** is on and you choose MP4, the export
  modal warns you and the background flattens to black.
</Note>

## Where PNG sequences go

Two destinations:

- **Download a .zip** (default) — works in every browser, no permission prompt
- **Write into a folder** — asks for a folder, then streams frames straight to
  disk. The only option with no practical size limit.

A zip is assembled in memory, so for very large sequences choose the folder.
The modal warns you when the estimate gets big enough to matter.

## Reading the estimate

The **est. size** figure is a rough projection, not a measurement — actual size
depends on how much movement is in the frame. Treat it as an order of magnitude,
mainly as a signal for whether you're about to ask the browser for something it
can't hold.
