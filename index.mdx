Brand Studio is a browser tool for making branded visuals. Two procedural
generators, a keyframe timeline, and exports that drop straight into a deck, a
website, or an editing timeline.

<Card title="Open Brand Studio" icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" href="https://mintlify-brand-studio.vercel.app" horizontal>
  Nothing to install. Works best in Chrome or Edge.
</Card>

## What you can get out

| Format | Alpha | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MP4 (H.264) | No | Web, Slack, decks, social |
| ProRes 4444 `.mov` | Yes | Dropping into After Effects or Premiere |
| PNG sequence | Yes | Long clips, or anything too big for memory |
| PNG still | Optional | Slides, thumbnails, static backgrounds |
| SVG | Yes | Print and web, where you need vector |

Everything renders at exactly the composition frame rate — 60fps by default —
no matter how choppy the live preview looks on your machine.

<Note>
  Your work autosaves to the browser, so a refresh won't lose it. For anything
  you want to keep or hand off, use **save** in the timeline bar to download a
  project file.
</Note>
